# Conceptual Framework

```text
RURAL JUSTICE ACCESS
    ├── Geographic accessibility
    ├── Economic affordability
    ├── Legal information
    ├── Legal assistance
    ├── Procedural accessibility
    ├── Digital inclusion
    └── Institutional responsiveness
            │
            ▼
DISPUTE RESOLUTION EXPERIENCE
    ├── Time to resolution
    ├── Financial burden
    ├── Procedural fairness
    ├── Legal certainty
    ├── Effective remedies
    └── Execution or implementation
            │
            ▼
RURAL HOUSEHOLD AND COMMUNITY WELLBEING
    ├── Asset security
    ├── Livelihood continuity
    ├── Household financial security
    ├── Access to public services
    ├── Community resilience
    └── Trust in institutions
```

## Methodological Guardrail
- **Correlation != Causality:** Observed associations between justice access indicators and household wellbeing do not establish causal impact without rigorous longitudinal or quasi-experimental evidence.
- **Judgment != Remedy:** A court judgment alone cannot establish household income, poverty reduction, livelihood continuity, or actual enforcement/execution of a remedy.
