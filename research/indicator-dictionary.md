# Candidate Indicator Dictionary

## Candidate Indicators for Future Rural Justice Access Index (RJAI)
1. **Geographic distance to justice services** (`IND-01`, km via verified route vs. straight-line)
2. **Estimated travel time** (`IND-02`, minutes/hours by primary local transport)
3. **Availability of legal aid** (`IND-03`, accredited OBH/PBH, Posbakum Pengadilan, Posbankum Desa)
4. **Availability of legal information** (`IND-04`, official JDIH, court info, village notice boards)
5. **Legal information accessibility** (`IND-05`, plain Indonesian, local language, offline formats)
6. **Cost burden of accessing services** (`IND-06`, official fees + transport + assistance vs. local income)
7. **Awareness of legal rights** (`IND-07`, survey-based, gated)
8. **Availability of offline services** (`IND-08`, *sidang keliling*, in-person Posbakum, paralegals)
9. **Digital connectivity** (`IND-09`, mobile/internet signal, BPS IP-TIK / PODES connectivity)
10. **Accessibility for persons with disabilities** (`IND-10`, physical & informational accommodations)
11. **Availability of local dispute resolution** (`IND-11`, village mediation / *lembaga adat* / *Bhabinkamtibmas* where documented)
12. **Reported barriers to resolving disputes** (`IND-12`, survey-based, gated)
13. **Access to effective remedies** (`IND-13`, execution/implementation tracking where evidence exists)

## Index Governance Gate
The **Rural Justice Access Index (RJAI)** will **NOT** be calculated or published until:
- Dimensions are validated;
- Indicators have defensible operational definitions;
- Data coverage and missingness are assessed and disclosed;
- Weights are justified and sensitivity analysis is performed;
- Independent human review is completed.

*Villages or districts must never be ranked using incomplete or non-comparable data.*
