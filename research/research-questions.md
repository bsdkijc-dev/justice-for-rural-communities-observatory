# Research Questions & Objectives

## Primary Research Question
> **How does access to justice shape the wellbeing and economic security of Indonesia's rural communities?**

## Ten Core Research Objectives
1. Measure geographical barriers (distance, travel time, transport availability) to formal and informal justice services.
2. Examine the affordability of formal and informal dispute resolution (official fees, transport, legal assistance, opportunity cost).
3. Identify recurring legal problems affecting rural livelihoods.
4. Examine land, agrarian, inheritance, family, and village governance disputes.
5. Assess access to legal aid (OBH/PBH, Posbakum, village paralegals) and legal information.
6. Investigate digital connectivity and digital legal literacy (including risks of digital exclusion).
7. Identify compounded barriers affecting women, older persons, persons with disabilities, and economically disadvantaged communities.
8. Examine how unresolved disputes may affect household assets, livelihoods, and economic security.
9. Identify practical opportunities for improving rural justice services (e.g., *sidang keliling*, *Posbakum Desa*, village mediation).
10. Develop evidence-based recommendations for accessible justice.

*Note: Research questions and objectives are investigative prompts, not findings. All empirical claims require verified evidence.*
