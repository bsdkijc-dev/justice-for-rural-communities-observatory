# Research Hypotheses

All hypotheses below are **untested propositions** to be evaluated against verified empirical evidence. They must not be reported as findings.

| ID | Hypothesis | Primary Datasets |
|---|---|---|
| **H1** | Greater geographic distance may be associated with lower use of formal justice services. | DS01, DS02, DS03, DS04 |
| **H2** | Higher access costs may be associated with delayed or abandoned dispute resolution. | DS03, DS06, DS08 |
| **H3** | Limited legal information may be associated with lower awareness of available remedies. | DS07, DS08 |
| **H4** | Access to legal assistance may be associated with improved understanding of procedures. | DS05, DS08 |
| **H5** | Digital access may improve legal information access, but may also exclude people with limited connectivity or literacy. | DS07, DS08, DS09 |
| **H6** | Unresolved land and livelihood disputes may be associated with economic insecurity. | DS04, DS08, DS09 |
| **H7** | Accessible mediation may reduce some dispute resolution burdens. | DS04, DS05, DS06, DS08 |
| **H8** | Rural communities may experience different access barriers depending on geography, gender, disability, income, and livelihood. | DS01, DS03, DS05, DS08, DS09 |
