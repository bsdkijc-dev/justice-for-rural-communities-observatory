# Privacy & Data Protection Policy

## 1. Personal Data Prohibition in Public Releases
- Never expose personal names, national IDs (NIK), phone numbers, addresses, precise household coordinates, or identifiable litigant, witness, judge, or staff information in any public dataset (`P1` or `P5`) or Kaggle/GitHub release.
- Never publish unapproved judicial documents or raw interview transcripts.

## 2. Survey & Interview Safeguards
- Separate respondent contact details from research response tables immediately upon collection.
- Use `anonymous_response_id` with no reversible linkage in analytical datasets (Dataset 08).
- Suppress or aggregate geographic cells with small counts (k-anonymity threshold) to prevent re-identification in remote or sparsely populated villages.

## 3. Compliance Reference
All data handling aligns with Indonesian personal data protection principles (UU No. 27 Tahun 2022 tentang Pelindungan Data Pribadi) and judicial anonymization standards.
