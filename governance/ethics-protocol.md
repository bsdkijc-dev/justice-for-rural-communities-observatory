# Ethics Protocol

## 1. Human Subjects & Vulnerable Populations
- Research involving rural households, women, older persons, persons with disabilities, informal workers, or remote/indigenous communities requires formal ethics review and explicit human approval prior to any field engagement, survey, or interview.
- Informed consent must be obtained in plain Indonesian or local languages, explaining voluntary participation, anonymity, and the right to withdraw without penalty.

## 2. Non-Fabrication & Do No Harm
- Never fabricate interview transcripts, survey responses, or community quotations.
- Do not provide individualized legal advice or guarantee legal outcomes in public knowledge materials; clearly distinguish general legal information from professional legal counsel.
- Avoid stigmatizing specific villages, courts, or officials; do not rank villages or districts using incomplete or non-comparable data.
