# Research Charter: Justice for Rural Communities Observatory

**Project:** Justice for Rural Communities Observatory (JRC Observatory)  
**Tagline:** Justice Within Reach. Stronger Rural Communities.  
**Parent Initiative:** MA Open Justice & Public Prosperity Initiative 2027–2030  
**Effective Date:** 2026-11-23  
**Status:** Independent Research and Public-Interest Knowledge Project (SAFE MODE)

---

## 1. Purpose and Mandate
The JRC Observatory exists to make the legal needs, access barriers, dispute resolution experiences, and institutional options of Indonesia's rural communities visible through verifiable, reproducible evidence. It does not portray rural communities as passive beneficiaries, nor does it substitute advocacy for empirical inquiry.

## 2. Institutional Independence
This project is an independent public-interest research initiative. It **does not** claim official endorsement, authorization, or institutional representation by Mahkamah Agung RI, Badan Pusat Statistik (BPS), Badan Pembinaan Hukum Nasional (BPHN), Kementerian Desa dan Pembangunan Daerah Tertinggal (Kemendesa PDT), or any other government agency.

## 3. Core Research Question
*How does access to justice shape the wellbeing and economic security of Indonesia's rural communities?*

## 4. Scope and Population
- **Primary Population:** Rural households, smallholder farmers, small-scale fishers, rural informal workers, village-based micro and small enterprises (UMKM), rural women, older persons, persons with disabilities, and communities in remote, border, island, mountainous, coastal, and peri-urban areas.
- **Secondary Institutional Population:** Courts and court service units, legal aid organizations (OBH/PBH), village administrations, local government service providers, and mediation/dispute resolution institutions.
- **Official Rural Classification:** Anchored to BPS urban/rural classification rules ([Peraturan Kepala BPS No. 37 Tahun 2010](https://ppid.bps.go.id/upload/doc/Perka_BPS_No_37_Tahun_2010___Klasifikasi_Perkotaan_dan_Perdesaan_Di_Indonesia_Buku_2_-_Jawa_1659514400.pdf)) and supplemented transparently by Kemendesa PDT's [Indeks Desa](https://sid.kemendesa.go.id/indeks-desa) ([Permendesa PDTT No. 9 Tahun 2024](https://jdih.kemendesa.go.id/web/regulations/read/indeks-desa-9-2024)) where village development status is analyzed.

## 5. Multi-Agent Governance & Human Approval Gates
All automated research and engineering agents operate under strict human-in-the-loop oversight. No agent may authorize its own publication. Explicit human approval is mandatory before:
1. Contacting institutions or individuals on behalf of the project.
2. Deploying surveys or conducting interviews.
3. Accessing restricted or authenticated systems.
4. Collecting personal or sensitive information.
5. Processing identifiable case records for public release.
6. Publishing datasets, reports, or websites (GitHub, Kaggle, or web portals).
7. Making claims about specific courts, judges, villages, or officials.
8. Spending funds or subscribing to paid services.
9. Deleting or overwriting source data.
10. Making any statement implying official institutional endorsement.
